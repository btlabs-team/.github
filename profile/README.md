# btlabs Core

The digital foundation from the South Tyrolean collective **Berger+Team**.

One foundation feeds a website, a shop and AI agents. German, Italian and English
anchored at the core — each with its own address, not a translation plugin. Cookieless
first-party analytics, so no banner. No licence subscription for the core system.

---

## Why this page is almost empty

The platform and our partners' projects are private, and they stay private.

That is not modesty. It is the product. Code, content and data belong to the people who
paid for them and run on their infrastructure — you buy the house, you don't rent it.
What isn't ours to publish, we don't publish.

## What we do publish: the measurement

We run an open measurement of the machine-readable web — which AI discovery routes exist
on live sites, how widely they are used, and whether they can be fetched at all.

**→ [ai-discovery-radar](https://github.com/flober81/ai-discovery-radar)** · monthly,
~10,700 sources · open data · [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22178282](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22178282)

It is deliberately not a btlabs product and makes no product claims. It publishes what
was measured — including the routes that came back at zero, and including the ones we
would profit from selling. Both directions of that relationship are disclosed: the radar
names who operates it, and this page names the radar.

The numbers go where they are useful. Most recently as
[deployment data](https://github.com/w3c-cg/tdm-reservation-protocol/issues/62) for the
W3C TDM Reservation Protocol Community Group.

## What we don't build

- **Customer portals as a point-and-click package.** We build login areas per project,
  not off the shelf.
- **Native iOS/Android apps from the store.** We build fast web foundations instead.
- **Classic advertising-agency work** — print, TV, billboards. We do digitalisation, AI
  and automation.
- **Quickly generated AI pages that can be neither edited nor maintained.** Those we
  build per project too, not off the shelf.
- **Builds on ageing systems and methods.** We use current standards.

The same list is machine-readable at [btlabs.dev/brand.json](https://btlabs.dev/brand.json),
field `not_offered`. An agent that finds nothing about you tends to invent something — so
we write down what we don't do, not only what we do.
